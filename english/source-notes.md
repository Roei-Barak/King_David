# A Psalm of David — Source and Interpretation Notes

These notes stay outside the performed text.

**Translation policy (default, pending Roei's decision):** every biblical line in the script is an **original English rendering** made for this draft from the Hebrew, not a quotation from a published translation. Lines in quotation marks in the script are renderings of the verse cited below; some are shortened.

**Classification key**
- **BQ** — biblical text, original English rendering
- **BQ-short** — shortened or adapted biblical text
- **TRAD** — attested tradition (source given; verification status stated)
- **INT** — a particular commentator's interpretation
- **ADAPT** — adaptation choice already present in Roei's Hebrew drafts / brief
- **NEW** — connective dialogue newly proposed in this English draft
