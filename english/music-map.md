### Notes for the composer / Roei
- The **"Lema'an Achai" phrase is the Saul–David motif**. The Hebrew draft proposes its return as an echo in Scene 8 (the women's song / Saul's jealousy). Its first line is deliberately the last thing David sings before Saul sleeps.
- Psalm 122 in full speaks of Jerusalem and "thrones of the house of David." Only vv. 8–9 are used here; this is a theatrical placement, not a claim about when the psalm was composed.
- The "I Want" song should not use up Psalm 23: "Gam Ki Elech" is reserved for the advance toward Goliath.
