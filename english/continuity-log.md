## Continuity established in Scenes 1–3 (to honor later)
- **Levana**, the strong ewe; David "stands in the gap."
- **Eliab** was looked at first and set aside; he heard the word "rejected"; he fetched David; he passed him bread without looking at him. → feeds his outburst in Sc. 4 (17:28).
- **The family is sworn to silence** about the anointing; Saul would kill Samuel "and not stop with him."
- **David: "He is the LORD's anointed"** / Samuel: "Remember that you said it." → cave (Sc. 18), Hachilah (Sc. 21).
- **Samuel's counsel not to raise a hand against Saul is explicitly not prophecy.**
- **Jesse:** "Beside me"; "Eat something on the road." The relationship has begun to mend and is not mended.
- **David's mother** kept the secret to protect Jesse's name (proposed motive).
- **Zeruiah** is the one David turns to for the blunt truth. → her sons (Joab, Abishai, Asahel).
- **Saul and David share:** the "Lema'an Achai" phrase; the laugh; the donkeys/sheep; "Did you want it?" (never answered); Saul's "I would have liked that. To be let off."
- **Saul told David his own anointing story** — David knows exactly what Saul lost.
- **David has heard Saul quote 1 Sam 15:28** ("your neighbor, who is better than you") and stayed silent.
- **The spear:** David has once taken it from Saul's sleeping hand and set it aside. Saul noticed and said nothing.
- **David is Saul's armor-bearer "in this hall"**; Saul himself sent him home when the war began. Saul knows him — the post-Goliath question (17:55) must not play as forgetting.
- "Come when I do not send." / "I will come."
- The lion and the bear have **not** been mentioned yet (held for the armor scene).
